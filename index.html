Are these real tickets on real airlines?

Yes. We book all major airlines, and you fly in the airline's own business class cabin. Your advisor tells you the airline and the flights for every option before you book.

Why is it less than the airline's website?

The airline's website shows its published fare. Our advisors also work with dozens of booking partners and several consolidators, which gives them thousands of special fares that are not listed publicly.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. Sending a request costs nothing and commits you to nothing. The form does not ask for payment details.

How far ahead should I ask?

As early as you like. We are booking through 2027 now. If you are joining a cruise or a tour, send the request once you know the dates.

What if I need to change or cancel?

You call your advisor and we handle it with the airline. Change and cancellation fees are set by the airline or the booking partner. Your advisor tells you what a change would cost when they find you the flight, so you know before you book.

How do I pay?

By Visa, Mastercard, American Express or bank transfer. We do not charge anything extra for any of them.

Will someone pressure me to book?

No. Your advisor finds the options and explains them, and the decision is yours. One client described it in a Google review as “Not a pushy agent, just one that stays in touch and follows your requests.”

Do you book domestic or short flights?

No. We book international flights of six hours or more.
